Here's a list of my favorite games and why!

  1. Megaman 3: Megaman 3 is one of my favorite games because it feels a little more fleshed out compared to MM2. I also really like the robot masters. My favorite is Snake Man. Not only does it feel more fleshed out but I really like the music that's in that game as well.
  2. Bravely Default: Bravely default is also one of my favorite games because it's one of the first RPGs that I've played and fell in love with the story for. I won't spoil anything, but I highly recommend playing that game if you ever have the time and a 3DS. I'm currently playing Bravely Default 2, which is actually the 3rd game.
  3. Final Fantasy 1: I really like RPGs and this is where it all began with the hit series Final Fantasy. There's something extra special about this game. Even though I had to use a walkthrough to figure out where to go a lot of the time, the gameplay was amazing and the final boss was really cool! Highly recommend playing this.
